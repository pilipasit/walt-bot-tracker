What the bot did

    How the bot decides
    1. Once every 4 hours, when a 4-hour bar closes (00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 UTC).
    2. It compares the fast average (last 20 closes) with the slow average (last 100 closes).
    3. Fast above slow: it holds a long. Fast below slow: it holds a short.
    4. When the lines cross, it closes the old position and opens the opposite one. Crossings are the only moments it trades.
    5. Each entry: 20 USD margin at 3x, stop-loss 3 % away, held by Walt; no take-profit. After a stop-loss it waits for the next crossing.
