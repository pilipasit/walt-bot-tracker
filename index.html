The rule

  1. The bot looks at the market once every 4 hours, when a 4-hour bar closes (00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 UTC).
  2. It compares two averages of the closing prices: the fast average (last 20 bars) and the slow average (last 100 bars).
  3. Fast above slow: it wants to be LONG. Fast below slow: it wants to be SHORT.
  4. When the two lines cross, it closes the old position and opens the opposite one. That crossing is the only moment it trades.
  5. Every entry gets a stop-loss 3% away, held by Walt. There is no take-profit. After a stop-loss, it stays out of that coin until the lines cross again.
