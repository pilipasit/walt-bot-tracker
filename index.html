Each card's own leverage menu overrides this. Cards are sorted by trend quality; the highlighted one has the strongest, cleanest (narrowest) trend right now.
How it's computed. Trend = the slope of the last 24 hourly closes (log scale) and how tightly prices follow it (R²), confirmed by a 12-hour average above/below a 48-hour one. Quality = drift per hour ÷ hourly noise × R², so a high score means a strong and narrow channel. Stop distance = 2.5 × the average hourly move + the channel's width; take-profit = 2 × the stop (reward:risk 2:1). Liquidation distance at leverage L ≈ 1/L − Walt's maintenance margin − fee (a touch closer than Walt's own figure). Fees use your Walt Silver tier: 0.07% per side on perpetuals (0.14% per round trip); funding isn't included. History: Binance hourly candles for crypto; Yahoo Finance hourly candles (unofficial; futures for commodities) for stocks, metals, energy, indices and FX. Levels use Walt's own price, sampled every 10 minutes. Rule-based arithmetic on past prices — not financial advice, and not instructions to the bot.
